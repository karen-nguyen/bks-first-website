Support the club

Sponsor STEMWorks

Five competition teams at James Martin High School run on donated materials, tooling, and entry fees. Sponsorship pays for the parts students build with, and your name goes on the machines they take to competition.

Become a sponsor See the tiers
Where the money goes

Materials and parts

Aluminum, carbon, motors, controllers, batteries, solar cells, airframes, and the spare parts that get used up in testing.

Entry and travel

Registration for the Solar Car Challenge, RoboBoat, FIRST, the American Rocketry Challenge, and the Bell drone competition, plus getting students there.

Tools and shop

Machining, fabrication, and electronics equipment in Room 140A that every one of the five teams shares.

Sponsorship tiers

Tier I

Under $500

  • Logo on website
  • Mention and logo in all presentations and marketing materials
  • Mention on social media

Tier II

$500 or more

  • A logo of your company will be displayed on one chosen vehicle
  • A logo of your company will be printed on a chosen t-shirt
  • Logo on website
  • Mention and logo in all presentations and marketing materials
  • Mention on social media

Tier III

$750 or more

  • A logo of your company will be displayed on three chosen vehicles
  • A logo of your company will be printed on three chosen t-shirts
  • Logo on website
  • Mention and logo in presentations and marketing materials
  • Mention on social media
  • Logo on team trailers

Power Sponsor

$1,500+

  • A logo of your company will be displayed on all vehicles
  • A logo of your company will be printed on all t-shirts
  • Special thanks and logo on website
  • Mention and logo in presentations and marketing materials
  • Mention on social media
  • Logo on team trailer

In-kind donations of material, tooling, machine time, or mentorship are welcome and are matched to the equivalent tier.
