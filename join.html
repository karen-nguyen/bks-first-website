Common questions

Do I need experience?

No. Most members join knowing nothing about the build and are trained by the team.

Can I be on more than one team?

Yes. You join STEMWorks, not a single team — the one $50 fee gives you access to all five. Talk to the captains about the time each season demands.

Does it cost anything?

Membership is a $50 club fee for the year, and it covers all five teams. Materials and entry fees are covered by sponsorship and fundraising. Ask a faculty sponsor if cost is a concern.

When can I join?

Any time. Teams are busiest during their competition season, but new members are taken all year.
